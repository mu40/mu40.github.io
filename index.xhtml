Brain imaging AI that works on any scan, out of the box

We develop deep-learning methods for brain segmentation and registration, which delineate anatomy and align it across images. Imaging AI often fails on unseen image types. We train models on wildly varied synthetic data so they work on new image types without retraining. Our tools are standard components of FreeSurfer, widely used brain MRI software developed at the Martinos Center for Biomedical Imaging.

I have advised companies building imaging AI on model robustness, segmentation, and registration. We also welcome sponsored research partnerships, for example to adapt our tools to new applications.

Software⬏

  • SynthStrip

    Skull-stripping for any brain image, with a dataset of brain masks for 622 MRI, CT, and PET scans

    • synthstrip.io
    • 28k+ Docker pulls
    • Default in FreeSurfer and Infant FreeSurfer

  • SynthMorph

    Rigid, affine, and deformable brain registration across scan types, without preprocessing

    • synthmorph.io
    • 12k+ Docker pulls
    • Default in FreeSurfer

  • BabySeg

    Brain segmentation for infants and young children from one or more MRIs of variable resolution and type

    • babyseg.io
    • 8k+ Docker pulls
    • New in Infant FreeSurfer
